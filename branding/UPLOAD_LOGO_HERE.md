# Logo Upload Instructions

1. Upload the main app logo as `branding/app/bq-sqwad-data-receipt-logo.png` (square PNG, 1024x1024 or larger, transparent background preferred).
2. Optional monochrome: `branding/app/bq-sqwad-data-receipt-logo-mono.png`.
3. After upload, run `./gradlew processBrandLogos` (or rebuild) to regenerate all icons and assets.
