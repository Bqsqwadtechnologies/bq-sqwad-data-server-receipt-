# Privacy Policy — BQ Sqwad Data Receipt

**Last updated:** 30 October 2026  
**Operator:** BQ Sqwad Technologies

## Who we are
BQ Sqwad Technologies (“we”, “us”) operates the BQ Sqwad Data Receipt mobile application.

## What the app does
BQ Sqwad Data Receipt shows the estimated monetary cost of your mobile data usage by reading Android’s own usage statistics on your device. No account is required.

## Data we process
- **On-device only:** Network usage statistics (bytes per app), bundle settings you enter, and preferences. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
- **Permissions:** Usage Access (to read Android NetworkStats), Notifications (alerts), Display over other apps (optional Live Cost Bubble), Internet (for ads only).
- **No VPN, no packet inspection, no browsing history.**

## Advertising
We use Google AdMob. Google may collect the advertising ID and device data according to Google’s policies. You can manage consent via the in-app “Privacy & ads choices” screen (UMP).

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Your rights
Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, GDPR and similar laws you may request information or deletion. Uninstalling the app or using in-app Reset removes local data.
