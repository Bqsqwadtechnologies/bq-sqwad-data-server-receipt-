Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
Operator: BQ Sqwad Technologies

Who we are

We are BQ Sqwad Technologies. We operate the BQ Sqwad Data Receipt mobile application.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, please contact us at hello@bqsqwadai.com.

What the app does

BQ Sqwad Data Receipt helps you understand the real cost of your mobile data. It shows how much each app is costing you in money, predicts when your data bundle will run out, and creates clear, shareable Data Receipts.

No account required

You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the app. Everything stays on your device.

Information we process

We process the following information only on your device:

  • Network usage statistics provided by Android (how much data each app has used)
  • The data bundle details you enter (price, size, validity)
  • Your preferences (country, currency, theme, alerts)

This information is never uploaded to our servers. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal usage data.

Permissions we request

  • Usage Access – Required so the app can read Android’s network usage statistics and calculate costs. We only read the statistics Android already keeps; we never look at the content of your internet traffic or the websites you visit.
  • Notifications – So we can alert you when your data is running low or when an app is using data in the background.
  • Display over other apps (optional) – Only used if you turn on the Live Cost Bubble.
  • Internet – Used solely to show ads (Google AdMob) and to open our privacy policy or other pages if you choose to view them online.

We do not use a VPN and we do not inspect the content of your traffic.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information and the advertising identifier according to its own privacy policy. You can manage your advertising choices inside the app under Privacy & ads choices.

Children

Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR, and similar laws, you have the right to know what information is processed about you and to request its deletion. Because all data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or by using the Reset option inside the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
